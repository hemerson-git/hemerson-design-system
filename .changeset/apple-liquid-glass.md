Glass now looks like Apple's Liquid Glass: a lighter tint (30% in dark, 20% in light) so the content behind shows through, a lighter blur, a lit two-tone rim, an inner glow and a text shadow for legibility. In Chromium, `enableGlassRefraction()` now bends the backdrop at each element's edges with a lens sized to it, replacing the noise distortion, and it is also available without React from `@hemerson/design-system/glass`. Dialog and toast text now uses the design system font.
